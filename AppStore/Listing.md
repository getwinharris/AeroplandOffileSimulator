## Subtitle (30 chars)

## Description
> A giggly, super-simple aeroplane game made for 3–5 year olds!
> Move the MOUSE to steer your cute smiley plane over a sunny island,
> collect shiny stars, whoosh through orange rings, and hold SHIFT to BOOST
> with a rainbow trail!
>
> - 6 colourful planes to pick: jets, biplane, glider, jumbo & rocket
> - Zero reading needed — big buttons, happy sounds, voice cheers
> - 100% OFFLINE — play in the car, plane, or grandma's house
> - Kid-safe: no ads, no chat, no purchases, no data collected, can't crash!
>
> Perfect first flight game for toddlers, preschool & kindergarten.
